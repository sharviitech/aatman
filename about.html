About Aatman & Nupur

A quiet space, built by someone who listens for a living.

Aatman began with a simple observation: most people already carry their answers. What they rarely have is a space calm enough to hear them.

Nupur’s story

[Placeholder] Nupur’s path into this work began with conversations — with friends, with strangers, with people in the middle of difficult chapters who simply needed someone to sit with them while they thought out loud.

[Placeholder] Over the years she trained in and worked with a range of reflective practices, gradually shaping an approach that is grounded, practical and deeply personal.

What led her to this work

[Placeholder] A belief that guidance should never feel like being told what to do — that the most useful thing anyone can offer is attention, honesty and a space free of judgement.

What Aatman means

[Placeholder] Aatman speaks to the self — the inner voice we tend to lose track of in noise and hurry. The work here is about returning to it.

Philosophy

Clarity, not prediction.

Nothing here is about certainty or fortune-telling. It is about seeing your situation more clearly, understanding your own responses to it, and choosing your next step deliberately.

Principles

  • Listen without judgement
  • Create space for reflection
  • Encourage self-awareness
  • Respect individual choices
  • Focus on clarity rather than prediction
  • Keep conversations personal and confidential
  • Help people discover their own direction

How Nupur works

A conversation, shaped around you.

Tarot Reading

Tarot used as a reflective tool — a way to explore a situation, its perspectives and the possibilities in front of you.

Counselling Conversations

One-to-one conversations that offer a safe space to express thoughts, explore situations and gain perspective.

Intuitive Guidance

A reflective approach that helps you connect with your own thoughts, feelings and inner perspective.

Personal Guidance

Tailored conversations shaped entirely around your situation, your questions and what you need right now.

Why people choose Aatman

Because it feels safe, unhurried and honest — a place to think clearly without being judged, rushed or sold a certainty that doesn’t exist.
